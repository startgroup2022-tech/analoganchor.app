04 iOS Translation & Brief Creative Freedom
STRATEGIC CONTEXT & CLIENT PROFILE
Our primary commercial audience is predominantly iOS-based and heavily female/ladies. They seek mindful calm, elegant life architecture, and intentional self-sovereignty—not aggressive gamer hardware aesthetics.
Creative Latitude for iOS Developer / Designer:
1. Visual Softness & Tonal Elevation
Do not feel constrained to duplicate Android's high-contrast dark obsidian. Feel completely free to explore warmer taupe, subtle champagne/rose tints, or an ultra-clean light mode with Apple's native .ultraThinMaterial frosted glass.
2. Native Apple Screen Time API
Replace Android's Device Admin & Accessibility Service with native Apple frameworks: FamilyControls, ManagedSettings, and DeviceActivityMonitor for battery-efficient system-level app shielding.
3. Apple Ergonomics & Dynamic Island
Leverage Live Activities & Dynamic Island for the floating focus timer pill. Integrate NFC Shortcuts, Action Button triggers, and Apple Watch companion taps for seamless tactile rituals.
4. Core Behavioral Invariants
The foundational mechanics must remain uncompromised: Physical Grounding (NFC / motion), Non-Reversible Pre-Commitment (locked anchors), and Sacred Circadian Pauses.
